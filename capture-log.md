# Capture log

The inbox. Things noticed but not yet processed into the database or the knowledge base.

A capture is a pointer, not a finding. It exists so that something spotted mid-session does
not derail the session and does not get lost. Processing it means one of three outcomes:
it becomes observations in the database, it becomes or revises a knowledge entry, or it
gets struck through with a note saying why it was dropped.

Keep this file short. A capture log with forty open items is a to-do list nobody is doing,
and the right response is to process or drop in bulk rather than let it grow.

## Format

```
- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD  <what it is, where it came from, why it might matter>
```

Mark processed items `[x]` with a note pointing at where they landed, and clear anything
older than a month that is still open. If it has sat for a month, it was not important.
